Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 12, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the WHOOP Performance Dashboard ("we", "our", or "the Application") collects, stores, and handles your personal data when you connect your WHOOP account.

🔒 Privacy Commitment: This application operates entirely locally on your device. We do not run any central servers, and your physiological data is never transmitted to us or any third parties.

1. Data We Access

When you authorize the Application using the official WHOOP Developer Portal, we request access to the following physiological and health metrics from your WHOOP profile:

  • Basic Profile: First name, last name, and email address (for dashboard personalization).
  • Body Measurements: Height, weight, and maximum heart rate (to display user metrics).
  • Physiological Cycles: Day strain, average heart rate, and caloric burn.
  • Sleep Data: Sleep performance, stages (Deep, REM, Light, Wake), and sleep consistency.
  • Recovery Scores: Daily recovery percentages, heart rate variability (HRV), resting heart rate (RHR), blood oxygen levels (SpO2), and skin temperature.
  • Workouts & Activities: Workout strain, sport type, average/maximum heart rate, and duration.

2. Data Storage

All data retrieved from the WHOOP API is stored in a local SQLite database (whoop_data.db) and local token files (tokens.json) in the Application's installation directory on your local machine.

  • Your data remains strictly on your own physical computer.
  • No cloud databases, external APIs (except the official WHOOP API), or third-party analytics trackers are used.

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, trade, or share your personal physiological data with any third-party company, advertising network, or service provider.

4. Data Retention and Deletion

Your local database will store historical data for as long as the Application is installed on your computer. You can delete all your stored data at any time by:

  • Deleting the whoop_data.db and tokens.json files from your local storage.
  • Revoking the Application's OAuth access via the WHOOP Member Portal under your App Integrations settings.

5. Changes to This Policy

Since this Application runs locally and does not collect your contact details, we will notify you of any policy changes by updating this page within the Application distribution.

6. Contact

If you have any questions about this Application or its local privacy practices, you can contact the developer directly at your registered email address.
